Chess Variants

Classic chess, the variants from Lichess and Chess.com, the ancient games chess grew from, and a few new ideas. Every one has bots from Easy to Impossible, or play friends on the same device.

Play online

Variants marked Stockfish use Stockfish on Impossible; the rest use the strongest built-in engine. Chess.com also hosts dozens of community variants, such as Spell Chess, Seirawan and Capablanca, that are not included here.
