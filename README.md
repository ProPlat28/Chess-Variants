# Chess Variants

A single-page chess game with 27 ways to play: classic chess, the variants from Lichess and Chess.com, the ancient games chess grew from, and a few custom ideas. Every variant has bots from Easy to Impossible, a 2-player mode on one device, and online play with friends through private room codes. It works on PC and phone.

**Play:** https://proplat28.github.io/Chess-Variants/

**Custom ideas**

| Variant | Idea |
|---|---|
| Secret Queen | One of your pawns is secretly a queen. It moves like one, and turns into a real queen a turn after it first moves. |
| Impostor | Secretly pick one enemy piece (not the king). Move it on your turn and it switches to your side. |
| King Capture | No check: kings can walk into danger and capturing a king wins. |
| 1, 2, 3 Moves | White plays 1 move, Black 2, White 3… A check ends the turn and restarts the count. |

**Special bots** (offline only)

| Mode | Idea |
|---|---|
| vs ChatGPT | A parody bot that plays normal chess, then teleports pieces, steals yours and refuses checkmate. Not affiliated with OpenAI. |
| vs Mirror Bot | Copies every move you make, even your checks: give check and it checks you back. |

Each variant has a Rules button in the game.
